this is apple jack


Applejack is a female Earth pony and one of the main characters of My Little Pony Friendship is Magic. She lives and works at Sweet Apple Acres with her grandmother Granny Smith, her older brother Big McIntosh, her younger sister Apple Bloom, and her dog Winona. She represents the element of honesty. she is bold, brave and honest.


develpoment and design

Lauren Faust was inspired to create Applejack by G1 Applejack.[1] Applejack is the only one of the Mane Six whose name, color scheme, and cutie mark didn't change from Faust's preliminary character sketches. The only difference in Applejack's final design is the addition of her cowboy hat. A character with the same name also appears in G3, but there are no design details that carried over to the G4 design. Along with Spike, Applejack is the only main character that Faust was able to carry over from G1 without changes to their name or design.
